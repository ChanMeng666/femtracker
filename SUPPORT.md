# Support

## Getting Help

If you need help with FemTracker, here are the available channels:

- **GitHub Discussions**: for general questions, feedback, and ideas, visit the [Discussions](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/femtracker/discussions) tab.
- **GitHub Issues**: for bug reports and feature requests, [open an issue](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/femtracker/issues).

## Common Questions

**The app won't start with `npm start`**
- Make sure dependencies are installed (`npm install`) and you have the [Expo](https://expo.dev/) tooling available.
- Use the Expo Go app or an iOS/Android simulator to open the project.

**Reminders/notifications aren't firing**
- Confirm the app has notification permissions on your device.

**Where is my data stored?**
- Usage data is handled on-device; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for our privacy and security stance.

## Scope of Support

This is an open-source project maintained on a volunteer basis. We do our best to respond, but
cannot guarantee response times. Please be patient and respectful.

## Security Issues

For security-related concerns, **do not open a public issue**. Follow the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
